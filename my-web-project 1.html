About us

We are a small coffee shop built around one idea: a good cup should feel like being welcomed home.

Our specialty coffee is 100% Arabica, pulled fresh and served with oat, almond, or coconut milk if you prefer. Alongside it, we pour Moroccan mint tea the traditional way and bake cookies, breads, and cinnamon rolls every morning.

Stay for a pour over and a book, or take a brownie for the road.
